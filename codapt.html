CoDAPT / Robust signal classification

Robust Drone Radio Frequency Fingerprint Identification via Confusion-Guided Adaptive Post-Training

Post-training that targets the class pairs an RF fingerprint classifier is most likely to confuse under noise.

Research question

How can an existing classifier recover accuracy under noisy conditions while preserving performance on clean signals?

Approach

CoDAPT identifies class pairs that become hard to distinguish at different signal-to-noise ratios and uses those confusions to guide classifier post-training. The method retains the classifier’s inference architecture.

Evaluation

The manuscript evaluates classification across signal-to-noise ratios and examines the trade-off between noise robustness and clean-signal accuracy.
