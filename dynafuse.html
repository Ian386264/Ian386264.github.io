DynaFuse / Image fusion

Where Tension Finds Balance: State-Adaptive Dynamics for General Image Fusion

A wavelet-domain framework that combines complementary images through explicit state evolution.

Research question

How can a single fusion framework combine source evidence when the inputs differ in contrast, exposure or focus?

Approach

DynaFuse separates coarse structure and directional details in a wavelet basis. It uses evidence from the source images to guide how fusion coefficients evolve before reconstruction.

Evaluation

The manuscript studies infrared–visible, multi-exposure, multi-focus and multimodal medical image fusion.
