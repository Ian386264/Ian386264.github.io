Counterfactual-Informed Distillation for FLAIR-Only Brain Tumor Segmentation

Shen Ye

Selective transfer from a multi-sequence MRI teacher to a model that segments brain tumours from a single FLAIR scan.

Read PDF · Author manuscript, 2026

Research question

When does information from additional MRI sequences help a segmentation model that will only have FLAIR at inference?

Approach

CFID estimates the usefulness of additional sequences at individual voxels by comparing a teacher’s predictions with and without those sequences. It uses the resulting estimates to guide selective knowledge transfer to a FLAIR-only model.

Evaluation

The manuscript evaluates the method on the BraTS 2023 development set and compares it with a FLAIR-only source model.
